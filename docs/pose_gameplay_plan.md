That progressive unlock idea is absolutely brilliant. Seriously, tying the physical photo-taking process into the tutorial missions turns what could have been a tedious setup screen into an interactive core gameplay mechanic. It gives the player immediate ownership of their character and avoids front-loading the friction. Top-tier game design right there.

Here is the comprehensive, full list of standard sprite poses, broken down into your proposed progressive phases.

Phase 1: The Movement Tutorial (Level 1)
This gets the player into the game as quickly as possible with the bare minimum needed to traverse a basic level.

Idle 1 & 2: \* Pose: Standing neutral, arms relaxed. Frame 2 is the exact same, but with the chest slightly puffed out as if taking a breath.

Walking 1 & 2: \* Pose: A standard marching pose. Frame 1: Left arm/Right leg forward. Frame 2: Right arm/Left leg forward.

Running 1, 2, & 3: \* Pose: More exaggerated than walking. Frame 1: Deep stride. Frame 2: Legs crossing center. Frame 3: Pushing off the back foot.

Jumping 1: \* Pose: Reaching upward, knees slightly tucked.

Falling 1: \* Pose: Arms slightly raised (flailing), legs pointing straight down to show gravity taking over.

Celebrate 1: \* Pose: Fist pump, arms raised in a V, or a peace sign. (Perfect for finishing the first tutorial level!)

Phase 2: Combat & Survival (Level 2)
Once they know how to move, it is time to introduce threats. Before they can hit an enemy, the game prompts them to take their combat photos.

Attacking 1 & 2: \* Pose: Frame 1 is the "wind up" (fist pulled back or weapon raised). Frame 2 is the "strike" (arm fully extended forward).

Taking Damage 1: \* Pose: The "flinch." Arms pulled up defensively to the chest, head tucked down, leaning slightly backward.

Defeat / Dead 1: \* Pose: Standing, but slumped completely over like a zombie, chin on chest, arms dangling straight down. (This keeps them centered in the camera mask without making them lie on the floor).

Phase 3: Advanced Mechanics (Later Levels/Metroidvania Unlocks)
These are situational. You only prompt the user to take these photos right before they encounter a level that strictly requires them.

Crouch / Duck 1: \* Pose: Squatting down, arms pulled in. (Requires a different camera mask that is lower on the screen).

Climbing 1 & 2: \* Pose: Reaching up vertically. Frame 1: Left hand high. Frame 2: Right hand high.

Using Item / Magic 1: \* Pose: Holding one hand straight up to the sky (like Zelda) or pointing directly forward with an open palm.

Sliding / Dashing 1: \* Pose: Leaning heavily forward, arms thrown backward (the classic anime run).

Pushing / Pulling 1: \* Pose: Both hands extended flat forward, leaning body weight into an imaginary block.
